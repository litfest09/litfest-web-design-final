Questions Answered

Common Questions

How do I book a village experience?

Simply browse our village experiences, select your dates and preferred activities, and complete a simple 3-step booking. Our village liaison will reach you within 24 hours to personalize your itinerary.

Is the experience safe for solo travellers?

Absolutely. All our village hosts are verified, background-checked, and culturally sensitized. We have a 24/7 traveler support line. Thousands of solo travelers — especially women — have experienced our villages safely.

How does my purchase support artisans?

85% of every product sale goes directly to the artisan. We operate on a transparent fair-trade model. You can track exactly where your money went via our impact dashboard after each purchase.

Can I visit as a group or family?

Yes! We offer group packages for families, schools, corporate teams and photography groups. Entire villages can be arranged for private cultural immersion retreats. Contact us for bespoke group planning.

Are the products on the marketplace authentic?

Every product carries a digital authenticity certificate linked to the artisan, their village, their craft tradition, and GI tag (where applicable). You receive the full story alongside your product.
